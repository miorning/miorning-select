# 视频封面 / Video covers

[下载三款六张封面与文案 / Download all six covers and copy](https://github.com/miorning/miorning-select/releases/download/v0.6.0/MiorningSelect-v0.6.0-covers.zip)

- A「到底用哪条？」：米白手绘，介绍选片用途。 / Ivory illustrated design about choosing a take.
- B「别来回切了，放一起比！」：亮黄毛绒猫，突出图片和视频同屏，推荐本版使用。 / Yellow plush-cat design highlighting image and video comparison; recommended for this release.

每款提供独立适配的横版16:9构图与4:3构图。原生PNG尺寸分别为1672×941（近似16:9）和1448×1086（精确4:3），未拉伸或重采样。包内附三套中文标题简介、完整生成提示词、离线预览和GPL许可。

Each concept has separately composed landscape 16:9 and 4:3 variants. Native PNG dimensions are 1672×941 (approximately 16:9) and 1448×1086 (exactly 4:3), without stretching or resampling. The archive includes Chinese titles/descriptions, full generation prompts, an offline preview and the GPL license.
